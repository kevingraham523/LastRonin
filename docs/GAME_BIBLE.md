## Authority and evidence labels

This document preserves the recoverable project, including all five story arcs. It does not invent missing story, maps, dialogue, boss phases, or numerical tuning. Full verbatim transcripts were not available through retrieval; 52 current project-folder files were inventoried, the latest production package and block source were read, selected art was visually inspected, and prior conversation context was retrieved. A complete approved screenplay and complete chapter list were not found.

- **LOCKED:** explicit current user instruction or recovered user decision.
- **RECOVERED PLAN:** prior narrative/design plan; detailed approval may not be recoverable.
- **PROTOTYPE:** behavior or content in an existing build; existence does not establish final design approval.
- **LEGACY:** earlier Hollow Signal experiment; reusable reference, not automatic campaign canon.
- **TBD:** missing, conflicting, or insufficiently specified.
- **PRODUCTION REQUIREMENT:** implementation/handoff criterion derived from the chosen direction, not a newly approved creative feature.

Current instructions override older prototype behavior: fixed orthographic camera, graphic-novel art, 3D characters, modular 2D environments. Keep the whole campaign in scope. The first production milestone is one complete playable city block; it is not the whole game.

## Locked project identity

Narrative action-adventure with an angled overhead orthographic presentation and a stylized graphic-novel look. Preserve the story-first focus and user-created 3D character assets. HTML5/JavaScript is the rapid playtest workflow before further Godot delivery. Exact shipping engine version and repository remain unverified; the user's classroom engine version is not evidence for this game's target.

The user intends to supply the final 3D characters. Temporary actors must be replaceable without redesigning missions or mechanics. All five arcs belong to the game; a city-block prototype is an integration milestone.

## Complete recovered story, across all five arcs

**Premise — RECOVERED PLAN.** Michelangelo survives the loss of his brothers and Splinter. The game follows the family he lost, the events that broke that family, his years of exile and training, his return to a future New York controlled by Oroku Hiroto and the Foot, and his final mission. Older April O’Neil, Casey Marie, the Resistance, and the brothers' memories connect his personal grief to the occupied city. Revenge, isolation, family, and the cost of carrying that loss are the central narrative concerns.

This is the latest recovered Last Ronin adaptation direction. The earlier original-identity Hollow Signal concept remains historical. Its corporations, districts, protagonist and Gatekeeper are not silently imported into this story. The published comics discussed in the project are reference material; discussing a comic event does not by itself approve that event as a playable mission.

### FAMILY — Before the Fall — The Family

**Recovered story:** establish Michelangelo's relationship with Raphael, Leonardo and Donatello before their deaths. Family training and teamwork supply the emotional baseline for the later loss.

**Recovered play intent:** playable training and teamwork with AI-controlled brothers. The player should experience the family relationship, not encounter the brothers only as names after their deaths.

**Participants:** Michelangelo and the three brothers. Splinter is part of the family story, but his exact scenes in this arc were not recovered.

**Known content:** MIS03 family training; MIS04 teamwork and relationships; LOC16 setting package.

**TBD:** exact location (do not assume a sewer lair), mission count, playable-character assignment, dialogue, family-era models, opponents, tutorial sequence, and transition to the losses. No new family adventure is written here.

### FALL — The Fall — Deaths of the Family

**Recovered story:** three loss packages explain Michelangelo's transformation: Raphael's final confrontation with Karai; Leonardo's sacrifice during Baxter Stockman's attack; Donatello and Splinter's last stand in Japan. These losses are central to the game rather than optional background.

**Recovered play intent:** playable flashback treatment was discussed. The controlled character in each scene and the balance of gameplay versus scripted outcome are unresolved.

**Known content:** MIS05 at LOC17; MIS06 at LOC18; MIS07 at LOC19.

**TBD:** exact sites outside the country-level Japan reference, order of presentation, encounter objectives, supporting cast, how each death is staged, and how inevitable narrative outcomes differ from retryable player failure. Karai is a confrontation participant; Baxter is associated with an attack. Neither statement authorizes an invented boss fight or boss phase list.

### LOST — The Lost Years — Becoming the Ronin

**Recovered story:** after the losses, Michelangelo goes into exile. Survival, grief, training and mastery of his brothers' weapons explain his development into the Ronin and his journey toward vengeance.

**Recovered play intent:** survival/exploration and weapon mastery through authored challenges. “Survival” is a story/play beat, not evidence for hunger, thirst, crafting or a survival sandbox.

**Known content:** MIS08 exile/survival; MIS09 mastery; MIS10 journey/grief; LOC20 setting package.

**TBD:** countries and named locations, elapsed years, mentors or additional characters, antagonists, individual trials, mastery order, mechanical rewards, and return transition. Do not fill this arc by automatically adapting all Lost Years comics.

### RETURN — Return to New York — The Revenge

**Recovered story:** Michelangelo returns to an occupied, monitored future New York controlled by Hiroto. Synja opposition, the changed city, and his relationships with older April, Casey Marie and the Resistance form the main return arc.

**Recovered play intent:** connected areas, deliberate combat and optional exploration/secrets. Streets, service alleys and roofs offer alternative traversal in the existing Outer District block. The older thematic outline described this as the largest connected area; no district count or open-world scope was fixed.

**Known content:** MIS11 occupied-city exploration/Synja combat; MIS12 ally/Resistance story; MIS13 optional exploration; LOC21–22. BLOCK01 is implemented return-era geography, though its exact narrative placement remains D01.

**Opening proposal:** a failed assault can frame the story before flashbacks. The project discussed the original comic's failed attempt on Hiroto and reunion with April. Exact mission adaptation, rescue staging, and protagonist-reveal timing were not recovered as approved scripts.

**TBD:** full district map, ally scenes and roles, Resistance objectives, assault-to-recovery sequence, optional rewards and preparation for the finale. No ally hub, rescue mission, vehicle chase or side quest is assumed merely because it would fit the setting.

### FINAL — The Final Mission — The Last Ronin

**Recovered story:** infiltrate Hiroto's stronghold, face escalating opposition, confront Hiroto, and deliver a bittersweet emotional conclusion to Michelangelo's journey.

**Known content:** MIS14 infiltration; MIS15 escalation; MIS16 final confrontation; MIS17 closure; LOC23–24.

**TBD:** approach, floors and interior rooms, enemy mix, boss phases, arena, playable versus cinematic resolution, exact character outcomes, final dialogue, epilogue and post-completion state. The approved adaptation's detailed ending was not recovered; it is not replaced here with an assumed comic ending or a newly invented ending.

## Weapons and emotional motifs

The recovered plan includes nunchaku, sai, bo staff and katanas associated with Michelangelo and his brothers. Weapon mastery belongs to LOST; exact unlock order, carrying rules, switching and combat differentiation remain TBD. Brother-memory effects were proposed, but there is no recovered specification making them summonable companions, stat buffs or powers.

The diner memory interaction in BLOCK01 is explicitly a draft story location, not approved dialogue or a completed flashback. Memory cues must support the established family story without introducing unapproved new events.
